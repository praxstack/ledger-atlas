1. Spec

There are two layers. The full vision is the money app you want to live in. Milestone one is the smaller contract we are allowed to claim as a candidate: importer, command line, public ledger commands, and a native Mac journey. Sync, cloud accounts, shipped iPhone or Android apps, telemetry, machine learning, and “we support Money Manager” are out of milestone one.

4. What is actually in Git

The local main branch contains product source and historical governance commits. The product-first workflow cleanup is committed as 62772b5. There is no configured remote, App Store delivery, or real-backup certificate.

Counts, not claims: about one fifth of the full vision; zero as a shipped app; milestone-one mostly present as a candidate; twenty-ticket program about two fifths as candidate and none closed except gate admission.
